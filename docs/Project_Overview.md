***# SM EventAura***



***## Full Name***

***SM EventAura – Smart Floral Decoration Business Management Platform***



***## Meaning***

***SM = Srinivasulu \& Madhavi***



***## Problem Statement***

***Many customers select decoration designs from Google, Instagram, or Pinterest that cannot always be recreated because of material availability or budget. Small floral decoration businesses also manage bookings, inventory, and customer details manually using diaries, leading to inefficiencies and lost opportunities.***



***## Objective***

***Build a web platform that helps customers explore real decoration designs, book consultations, request quotations, and helps decorators manage bookings, inventory, rentals, and customers efficiently.***
